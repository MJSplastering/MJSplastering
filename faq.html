FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Who will I be dealing with?

Myles. MJS Gyprock & Plastering is his own business, and MJS are his initials.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free and written.

Can I just send photos?

For some jobs, yes. Photos are enough to understand what's needed. For others, Myles will come out and have a look before quoting. It depends on the job.

How do I get in touch?

Call or text Myles on 0490 790 564, email info@mjs-plastering.com.au, or use the enquiry form. You can attach up to five photos to the form.

Where are you based, and where do you work?

MJS is based in Old Bar. Myles works across the Mid North Coast NSW: Port Macquarie to Forster, across to Gloucester, and everywhere in between.
